FRESH DESIGN SYSTEM:
Use warm-white #FAFBF7 app backgrounds, soft sage #E7EFE5 panels, very light powder-blue #E9F0F5 supporting surfaces, and deep desaturated green #416553 for primary controls. Text dark forest charcoal #293B32, secondary text #697D70. Pale ochre can mark a calendar conflict. Amounts use dark ink, never promotional red. Avoid loud warning colors except a genuinely necessary small status marker; cancellation is a quiet secondary action.
Typography is crisp modern Chinese sans-serif like Noto Sans SC, normal body weight, medium-weight titles, compact refined number typography. At the atlas scale, titles about 28–36 px, body 21–24 px, metadata no smaller than 18 px. Native controls should be easy to tap. Primary buttons are well-proportioned sage-green pills; secondary actions are understated text buttons or very pale filled pills, not blue outline rectangles. Use lots of generous breathing room and simple thin line icons. Cards use white surfaces, subtle soft shadow, 24-logical-pixel corners, excellent readable spacing. Prefer spacing to borders. No excessive inner boxes or card-within-card clutter.
