# OctoSense Camera privacy statement

Last updated 20 September 2026.

OctoSense Camera is an experimental, open source camera app. This statement
covers the HarmonyOS app with the bundle `dev.makepad.octosense.camera`.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no
crash reporting. It sends no personal information anywhere.

## What the app accesses on the device

- **Camera.** Used to show the preview and to take a photo or a video. Frames
  are processed on the device and are not transmitted.
- **Microphone.** Used only while recording a video, to record the sound of that
  video.
- **Photos and videos in the media library.** Used to save a photo or a video
  the person has just taken. The app writes the files it creates. It does not
  read the rest of the library.
- **Network.** The interface loads its own images from a connection to the
  device itself. No request leaves the phone.

## Where the photos go

A photo or a video is written to the device's own storage and handed to the
system gallery. It stays on the phone. The developer never receives it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
